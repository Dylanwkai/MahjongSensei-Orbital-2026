? The Problem

Mahjong has one of the steepest learning curves of any tile game. Beginners struggle with tile recognition, hand structure, scoring, and above all deciding what to discard.

Most learners also have no experienced player on hand to explain why a move was good or bad. Without that feedback, bad habits stick and progress stalls.

! Our Solution

MahjongSensei combines guided lessons, live hand analysis, and hands on practice in one place. A learner moves from reading tiles, to understanding winning hands, to getting a recommended discard, to playing a full game.

A custom Python Mahjong engine powers the teaching, the analysis, and the computer opponents, so every feature speaks the same rules.

★ Core Features Built This Milestone

1

Interactive Tutorial

Three guided modules cover tile recognition, meld types, and winning hands. Each one ends in a quiz, and completion saves per user through a TutorialProgress model.
2

Hand Helper

Build a 14 tile hand with a visual picker and get the best tile to discard plus the reasoning. A HandEvaluator and ValuationAlgorithm test every possible discard.
3

Interactive Trainer

Get dealt a hand, choose a discard, and receive instant feedback scored against the optimal play. Every attempt is logged and running accuracy is tracked.
4

Solo Play

Play a full game against three AI opponents. The engine handles dealing, draws, flowers, concealed Kongs, self drawn wins, and the washout draw.
5

Accounts and Profile

Registration and login use JWT tokens. Protected routes survive a refresh by restoring the session, and a profile page holds the stats foundation.
6

Win Check and Tai Scoring

Detects standard, Seven Pairs, and Thirteen Orphans hands, then scores wins in tai under Singapore rules including winds, dragons, flowers, and flushes.

⚠ Problems We Solved

Trainer 500 error
Duplicate play sessions broke the trainer. We changed the logic to reuse the latest session instead of assuming only one exists.
Sessions looked logged out
A page refresh dropped the user. We restore the session by re fetching the profile and handling token expiry on the frontend.
Recurring API failures
Hard to trace errors turned out to be unapplied database migrations. Running migrations as part of setup fixed it.
